## Facts the next session needs

- **Build:** clean 7.5 s, incremental about 0.3 s (the `AGENTS.md` head). cargo needs the sandbox off, because its registry cache is outside it.
- **Tests need part of the corpus:** `python3 -I scripts/corpus/fetch.py --only blargg --only mooneye --only gbmicrotest --only dmg-acid2`. Without it, the tests fail and name the command. Agents must fetch it in their own worktree. Fetching through the sandbox needs `github.com`, `codeload.github.com` and `release-assets.githubusercontent.com` (plus `gekkio.fi` for the full corpus), or the sandbox off.
- **SameBoy is built `-O3 -DNDEBUG`.** At `-O2` with asserts on, SameBoy's own C loop couldn't reach the 18× target.
- **SameBoy can't report the PPU dot** (design §2.2 correction). Event sinks are `Unsupported` until the profiler leaf. The illegal-opcode pre-check costs about 8% throughput.
- **The C shim (`crates/sameboy-sys/shim/gbcli_shim.c`) mirrors parts of SameBoy's read paths** without their side effects. Re-check it on every re-vendor; its growth is the gauge of how hard we are fighting the core.
- **Licenses:** blargg's ROMs have no license (fetch only, never commit). Three third-party ROMs in docboy's suite also have none.
