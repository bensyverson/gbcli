## Rulings and corrections

- **`--until` cadence** (user confirmed option 1, 2026-11-06): derive triggers from the expression; the per-instruction baseline is the correctness floor. Two corrections during the build, both in design §8/§11:
  - A mixed predicate keeps its write candidates as well as the finer trigger; a pure "finest only" ordering misses a mid-frame write.
  - Every IO read and every raw CPU address in a switchable area take the per-instruction baseline (internal serial/APU/PPU writes are invisible to the write hook; a bank switch changes a mapped byte). Symbols and region forms stay on the cheap path.
- **`[rLY]` is not exactly evaluable** on SameBoy (`Unsupported(SideEffectFreeIo)` while mode-3 cycles are batched); `ly` is the exact scanline predicate.
- **The wall guard** answers `budget_exhausted` with `StopReason::WallGuard`, not `budget, kind: wall` (design §3 correction).
